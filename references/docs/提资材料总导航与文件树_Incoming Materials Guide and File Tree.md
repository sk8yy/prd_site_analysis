## English Guide

Use this guide in three steps: choose a question, open the relevant province/city brief, then follow a file or page reference to the original. The complete filename tree is included below; individual city briefs provide the detailed evidence.

### 1. Scope and Evidence

Snapshot: **20 September 2026**. **61 original files**: 37 text-bearing documents/tables/logs, 23 images and one video. The six geographic folders contain 59 files; two shared sources sit at the root. The seven AI guides are additional navigation documents, not original evidence. Editor/tool configuration folders are excluded.

All document files were parsed; contents, scope, selected substantive sections and tables were checked. All 23 images were viewed in labelled contact sheets. Some PDFs remain image-heavy: extraction does not mean every drawing or page was visually audited. The shared video is inventoried only, not watched or transcribed. No external checks of present operation, planning approval or legal validity were made.

English titles are explanatory translations, not official English names. Chinese filenames and folder names are retained exactly so links remain traceable. “Last three years” is an inherited folder label, not a verified date filter. Dates and construction status in the briefs belong to their respective sources.
